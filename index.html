The studio

A working atelier, not a showroom — where the drawing, the pattern and the last are all in the same room.

Ejesira Design Studio was founded in Addis Ababa in 2018 by Kasahun Hailu Eshetu, a leather product designer and industrial design expert with more than sixteen years in the Ethiopian footwear and leather goods industry.

The studio makes handmade shoes and leather bags, and designs them — two halves of the same practice. Nine people work here: the founder, three bag and sample makers, and five shoemakers. Every piece shown on this site was designed, developed or made by this studio.

Alongside its own collections, Ejesira works as a product-development partner to footwear manufacturers and leather enterprises — from first concept through pattern making and CAD development to production-ready samples.

Creative philosophy

Design, craftsmanship, culture, innovation and responsibility — treated as one practice, not five departments.

The studio works from the conviction that a drawing is only an opinion until someone can build it. So design here is tied to construction, material and the realities of a production line from the first sketch onward.

That comes from an unusual path: years of research and model-factory leadership at Ethiopia's Leather Industry Development Institute, a master's in creative design and CAD/CAM in India, and the daily discipline of a small atelier where the maker and the designer argue over the same pattern.

Materials are chosen locally wherever the quality allows, offcuts are cut into small leather goods rather than discarded, and pieces are built to be repaired and kept.
