How this works
  1. Edit anything below — studio details, products, collections, projects, journal entries.
  2. Preview changes opens the site in a new tab using your edits, so you can check before committing.
  3. Download content.js saves a new content.js file.
  4. Upload that file to your host, replacing assets/js/content.js. The site updates immediately.

To add a new photograph, upload the image to assets/img/ on your host, then type its filename into the Image field of a product or project. Square images on a white background work best for products.

Only publish what is true. Leave a field empty rather than inventing a specification, an award or a stock level.
